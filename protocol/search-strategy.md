# Search Strategy

## Databases
- IEEE Xplore
- ACM Digital Library
- Scopus
- Web of Science
- ScienceDirect
- SpringerLink
- Google Scholar (supplementary, first N results)
- AJOL – African Journals Online (regional coverage)

## Search String (base)

```
("edge computing" OR "fog computing" OR "mobile edge computing" OR "multi-access edge computing" OR "MEC" OR "edge intelligence")
AND
("Africa" OR "sub-Saharan" OR "Nigeria" OR "Kenya" OR "South Africa" OR "Ghana" OR "Egypt" OR "Rwanda" OR "Ethiopia" OR "developing countries" OR "Global South")
```

Adapt syntax per database and record the exact string used in `literature/search-logs/`.

## Limits
- Publication years: <!-- e.g., 2015–2026 -->
- Language: English (others noted if translatable)
- Document types: journal articles, conference papers, reports

## Supplementary Methods
- Backward and forward snowballing of included studies.
- Grey literature: GSMA, ITU, World Bank, AU reports.
