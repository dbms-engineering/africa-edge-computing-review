# Research Questions

- **RQ1:** What is the current state of edge computing research and deployment in Africa?
- **RQ2:** Which application domains and use cases are addressed (e.g., agriculture, healthcare, finance, smart cities, education)?
- **RQ3:** What architectures, platforms, and technologies are employed?
- **RQ4:** What challenges (infrastructural, economic, energy, regulatory, skills) affect adoption?
- **RQ5:** What research gaps and future directions emerge from the literature?

## PICOC Framing

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Population | Edge/fog computing systems, deployments, or studies in African contexts |
| Intervention | Edge, fog, or MEC (multi-access edge computing) approaches |
| Comparison | Cloud-centric or no-compute alternatives (where applicable) |
| Outcomes | Performance, cost, adoption, accessibility, reliability, energy |
| Context | African countries / regions |
