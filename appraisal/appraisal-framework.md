# Quality Appraisal Framework

Each included study is scored against the criteria below.
Scoring: **Yes = 1**, **Partly = 0.5**, **No = 0**.

| ID | Question |
|----|----------|
| QA1 | Are the aims and research questions clearly stated? |
| QA2 | Is the African context clearly described? |
| QA3 | Is the methodology appropriate and described in sufficient detail? |
| QA4 | Is the edge/fog architecture or system adequately described? |
| QA5 | Is the evaluation (experiment, case study, survey) rigorous? |
| QA6 | Are results clearly reported and supported by data? |
| QA7 | Are limitations and threats to validity discussed? |
| QA8 | Does the study contribute to research or practice? |

**Maximum score:** 8
**Quality bands:** High (≥ 6), Medium (4–5.5), Low (< 4)
