## Categories
- **Geographic gaps** – under-represented countries/regions
- **Domain gaps** – under-explored application areas
- **Methodological gaps** – e.g., lack of real-world deployments vs. simulations
- **Technical gaps** – energy-aware, offline-first, low-cost hardware
- **Policy gaps** – data sovereignty, regulation, spectrum
